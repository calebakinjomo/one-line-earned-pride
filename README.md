An entry for the [One Line of Earned Pride](https://make.fun/competitions/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557) bounty on make.fun.

`main.py` writes a single line — one quiet, earned moment of pride — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> At nine, after two years of sounding out every word, Sam read the bedtime story to his little sister all the way to the end, and she fell asleep before he did.

A small person doing something hard: two years of sounding out every word, then one bedtime story read all the way to the end. The pride sits in the image — his little sister asleep before he is — never in an adjective.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
